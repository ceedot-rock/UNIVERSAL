# Contributing to UNIVERSAL

UNIVERSAL is a living document: one text for all intelligence, kept honest
by the people who read it. Corrections are welcome; the bar is the document's
own covenant — checkable work, plain language, no performed certainty.

## Ground rules

- **Checkable claims.** Every factual claim needs a checkable source —
  an arXiv reference, a paper, a primary source. "Trust me" is not a source.
- **Acronyms and terms are expanded on first use.** Never assume the reader
  tracks the alphabet soup.
- **No license changes.** UNIVERSAL is CC BY 4.0, always. Do not touch
  the LICENSE file.
- **Match the voice.** Nine parts, one voice, zero personal notes.
  Propose edits that fit the document's register, not your own.
- **Small is better.** The document is ~17,500 words. Prefer precise
  fixes — a wrong citation, a muddled sentence — over new sections.
  Large additions should start as an issue for discussion first.

## How to propose a change

1. Open an issue describing the problem (the document-error template
   helps), or go straight to a pull request for small fixes.
2. Keep the change scoped: one problem per pull request.
3. Use the pull-request template.

## What makes a good contribution

- A broken or misattributed citation, fixed with the correct source
- A sentence the document's own readers keep misunderstanding
- A genuinely missing consideration the covenant's logic already demands

## What doesn't belong

- Personal essays, anecdotes, or named endorsements
- Claims about what the document's author(s) intended that aren't in the text
- Rewrites of the covenant itself without prior discussion
